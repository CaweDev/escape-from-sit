\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Defines a named animation as a range of frames within a spritesheet. 

Frames are indexed in row-\/major order (left-\/to-\/right, top-\/to-\/bottom), starting from 0 at the top-\/left cell of the spritesheet.

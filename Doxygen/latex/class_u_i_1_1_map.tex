\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Renders a large top-\/down view of the entire tile map. 

Designed for full-\/screen viewing. Unlike the \doxylink{class_u_i_1_1_minimap}{Minimap}, this class scales the entire level to fit within its \doxylink{namespace_u_i}{UI} bounds. It supports background textures, decorative borders, and displays the player\textquotesingle{}s position as a marker.

\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Marks a \doxylink{class_engine_1_1_game_object}{Game\+Object} as interactable when player looks at it. 

Attach this component to any \doxylink{class_engine_1_1_game_object}{Game\+Object} with a billboard \doxylink{class_engine_1_1_sprite_renderer}{Sprite\+Renderer} to make it interactable. The \doxylink{class_engine_1_1_interaction_controller}{Interaction\+Controller} will automatically detect it when the player looks at it.
